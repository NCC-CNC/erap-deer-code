Action recommendations
Area-based conservation – 30x30 goals
Recommended because current protection is less than 30%
Area-based conservation – endemic SAR
Recommended because protection goals for at least 1 SAR can only be met in this ecoregion
Area-based conservation – SAR
Recommended because protection goals for at least 5 SAR will likely include this ecoregion
Area-based conservation – biodiversity urgency
Recommended due to biodiversity urgency: more than 20% of the ecoregion is an optimal location for meeting Canada-wide biodiversity goals
Area-based conservation – biodiversity opportunities
Recommended due to biodiversity opportunity: more than 1M ha in the ecoregion are optimal locations for meeting Canada-wide biodiversity goals
Site / Area stewardship
Recommended because NCC is the management authority on projects and needs to manage or steward land to maintain or enhance landscape resiliency
Ecosystem & Natural Process (Re)Creation – 30x30 goals
Recommended because 30x30 goals cannot be met without restoring landscapes (existing protection plus intact land is <30% of the ecoregion)
Ecosystem & Natural Process (Re)Creation – opportunities
Recommended because the ecoregion is highly modified and there are lots of opportunities for landscape restoration projects
Alliance & Partnership Development – land sharing
Recommended because NCC owns fee-simple land and should consider appropriate land sharing strategies
